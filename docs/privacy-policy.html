Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Data Storage

Words, progress, settings and preferences stay on your device. To power translations, pronunciation, stories, chat and scan, the relevant text or photo is sent to our API. We do not keep scan photos after the request. Droword does not collect personal profiles on our servers.

AI Translation

When you add or translate a word, the text is sent to a third-party AI service (Anthropic Claude) to generate translations, explanations and examples. No personal information is included in these requests.

Text-to-Speech

Audio pronunciation is generated using the OpenAI Text-to-Speech API. Only the word or phrase text is sent — no personal data is transmitted.

Photos & Camera

If you set a profile image, it stays on your device. If you scan a page of words, that photo is sent to our API so we can read the vocabulary. We do not keep the image after the scan.

Notifications

Droword may send local notifications to remind you to practice. These are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any external service.

Analytics & Tracking

Droword does not use any analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. Your usage data stays on your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time from Settings → Dictionary → Clear All Words. Removing the app from your device deletes all stored data permanently.

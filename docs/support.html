Common questions

  • Where is my data? Words and progress stay on your device. AI features send only the text or photo needed for that request.
  • How do I cancel PRO? App Store → Account → Subscriptions → Droword.
  • How do I delete my data? Settings → Dictionary → Clear All Words, or delete the app.
  • Restore purchases from the PRO screen if your subscription doesn’t unlock.
